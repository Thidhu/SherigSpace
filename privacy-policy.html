SherigSpace is built to support the learning and teaching of the Dzongkha language. Please read this page carefully before using the platform.

1. About SherigSpace

SherigSpace is a digital platform focused on learning and teaching the Dzongkha language. It provides educational resources, interactive activities, learning materials, videos, exercises, and other tools designed to support Dzongkha learners, teachers, and educators.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SherigSpace, we may collect account information such as your name, email address, profile information, and authentication information. If you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive basic profile information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile picture, subject to the permissions you authorize.

3. Learning, Account, and Subscription Information

We may collect information needed to provide your account, learning access, subscriptions, purchases, saved progress, comments, uploaded materials, and other features you choose to use. Payment providers may process payment information on our behalf.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, provide Dzongkha learning features, authenticate users, provide free and premium resources, manage subscriptions and purchases, improve the platform, communicate with users, maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Google Sign-In

When you choose Sign in with Google, Google provides information necessary to authenticate your SherigSpace account according to the permissions you authorize. SherigSpace does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other Google services unless a future feature specifically requires that access and you authorize it.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

SherigSpace may provide paid subscriptions or premium Dzongkha learning resources. Where a third-party payment provider is used, payment card or banking information may be collected and processed by that provider rather than stored directly by SherigSpace. We may receive transaction information needed to confirm and manage your purchase or subscription.

7. User Content and Educational Materials

Users may be able to submit comments, teaching materials, assignments, or other content. Please do not submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information about students or other individuals. Teachers and institutions are responsible for ensuring that information they submit is authorized and handled in accordance with applicable requirements and school policies.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate SherigSpace, such as authentication, hosting, database, storage, analytics, email, security, and payment providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, SherigSpace, or others.

9. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal requirements.

10. Children and Student Information

SherigSpace is designed primarily for teachers, educators, and learners of Dzongkha. Users should not submit unnecessary personal information about students. Teachers, schools, and organizations are responsible for ensuring that their use of SherigSpace and any student information they submit is properly authorized and complies with applicable requirements and permissions.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SherigSpace may use cookies, local storage, sessions, or similar technologies to keep users signed in, maintain security, remember preferences, and understand service usage. Third-party services integrated with SherigSpace may use their own technologies.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

SherigSpace may use or link to third-party services, including Google authentication, hosting, storage, analytics, and payment services. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. We encourage users to review the policies of third-party services they use.

13. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. You may also ask questions about how your information is handled. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SherigSpace, our technology, or applicable requirements change. The Last updated date will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
